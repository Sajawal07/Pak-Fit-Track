Pak Fit Track — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Pak Fit Track (“we”, “our”, “the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address when you sign in (Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In)
  • Profile: name, age, gender, height, weight, goals, diet preferences
  • Fitness logs: food intake, weight, body measurements, workouts, water intake, and steps (only if you grant permission)
  • Optional analytics: app opens and feature usage via Firebase Analytics — off by default; you can enable it under Profile → Privacy & data

2. How we use data

We use your data only to provide calorie and nutrition tracking, personalize targets (BMR/TDEE), sync signed-in data across devices, and (if enabled) improve the app.

3. Storage & processors

Data is stored on your device (local database). When signed in, it is synced to Google Firebase (Firestore / Authentication) under your account. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Permissions

Optional permissions may include activity recognition / step counting and notifications for reminders. You can deny or revoke these in system settings.

5. Your choices & deletion

You can delete your account and cloud data anytime in the app: Profile → Delete Account. Guest data stays on the device until you clear app data or uninstall.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under the applicable age in your region.

7. Health disclaimer

Pak Fit Track provides general fitness and nutrition information for educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

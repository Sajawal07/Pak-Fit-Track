## Full Description
Take control of your health with Pak Fit Track — a Pakistan-focused nutrition and fitness tracking app designed for the South Asian diet and lifestyle.

**Track Your Meals & Nutrition**
Log every meal with detailed food tracking, calorie counting, and macronutrient breakdown. Set personalized calorie targets based on BMR and TDEE calculations.

**Monitor Your Weight & Progress**
Track your weight over time with visual charts and progress insights. Set weight loss goals and monitor your body measurements.

**Stay Active with Step Tracking**
Connect to Health Connect to track your daily steps and activity levels. Get insights into your physical activity patterns.

**Flexible Sign-In**
Sign in with Google or with your email/password. Your data syncs across devices when you're signed in.

**Your Data, Your Control**
Request account deletion anytime from Profile → Settings → Delete account. Deletion requests are processed manually by an administrator, and your privacy is protected with Firebase security rules.

**Offline First**
All data is stored locally on your device. Sync happens automatically when you're connected to the internet.

**Privacy Focused**
Analytics is off by default. No ads. No selling of your data.

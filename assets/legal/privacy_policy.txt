Pak Fit Track — Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026

INFORMATION WE COLLECT
• Account: email address (Firebase Authentication)
• Profile: name, age, gender, height, weight, goals, diet preferences
• Health & fitness logs: food intake, weight, body measurements, workouts, water intake
• Optional: step count (only if you grant Health Connect / activity permission)
• Analytics (optional, OFF by default): enable in Profile → Privacy & data. Events: app opens, onboarding, meal type when logging food
• Local device ID: random UUID stored on device for offline sync queue (not used for ads)

HOW WE USE DATA
To provide nutrition and fitness tracking, sync data when signed in, and improve the app.

STORAGE
Data is stored on your device (Isar database). When signed in, profile and logs sync to Google Firebase Firestore under your account.

SHARING
We do not sell personal data. Google Firebase processes data as our infrastructure provider.

YOUR CHOICES
• Disable analytics: Profile → Privacy & data
• Delete account: Profile → Delete account (removes Firebase Auth, Firestore data, and local logs on this device)
• Guest mode: data stays on device until you delete local data or uninstall

NOT COLLECTED
Location, contacts, photos, SMS, crash reports, push notification tokens, payment info, advertising ID.
